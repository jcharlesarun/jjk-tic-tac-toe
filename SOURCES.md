## Adaptation

Each fighter has one signature technique: Black Flash, Hollow Purple, Dismantle, Nue or World Slash. Attacks trigger automatically after a round win. Yuji's traveling energy strike is a visual adaptation rather than a literal ranged Black Flash from the source game. Yuji and Sukuna are separate selectable fighters here; Vessel combines them in the reference game. Mahoraga is independently selectable here rather than summoned through Megumi.
All techniques deal one hit. The source game's cooldowns, damage, adaptation mechanics, summons and combat systems are not reproduced. Visuals are abstract CSS effects.
